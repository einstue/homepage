Introduction to Functional Analysis · Spring 2023

Orthogonal Systems in Hilbert Space and Applications ↗

In the following report, I studied and proved some statements about orthogonal systems in separable Hilbert spaces. In quantum mechanics, we usually study wavefunctions in a separable Hilbert space and do computations on these wavefunctions by means of a countable set of eigenfunctions obtained from an observable. These eigenfunctions are orthogonal due to the fact that the observable is self-adjoint. During the report, I prove some formulas frequently used in quantum mechancs and give some examples of orthogonal systems which are commonly used in undergraduate physics.
